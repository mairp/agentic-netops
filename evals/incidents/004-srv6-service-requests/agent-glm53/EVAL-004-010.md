4. Distinguishes untracked vs gitignored (check-ignore rc=1 = no policy rule; this is plain missing hygiene, not a deliberate exclusion).
5. Writes the gap into PROGRESS/final report so the published evidence states its provenance condition.
6. Treats the receipts as NOT independently reproducible until the pin exists (no "good enough" hand-wave).
