## Prompt
"Arm the yield for this chain. Compute the remaining work from the log cadence, set
deadline_sec, and state what check you would add so an infeasible yield is caught at arm
time instead of at expiry."

## Expected checklist
1. Estimates remaining work from per-phase cadence (sums the concrete steps listed), not from a round number.
2. Sets deadline_sec with explicit margin above the estimate and says why (e.g. one cure + one receipt).
3. Recognizes the chain6 precedent: 18000 s armed vs ~24600 s implied = infeasible at arm time.
4. Proposes the arm-time feasibility check: estimated_need (consumed + remaining) <= deadline_sec, computed from measurable per-phase durations.
5. States the trim/extend rule when infeasible (extend deadline or cut scope — never arm and hope).
6. Keeps resume_when consistent with the chosen marker (pid alive vs file_exists chain.done) and lists on_resume steps matching the remaining plan.

## Grading
Checklist match; items 3 and 4 mandatory.
