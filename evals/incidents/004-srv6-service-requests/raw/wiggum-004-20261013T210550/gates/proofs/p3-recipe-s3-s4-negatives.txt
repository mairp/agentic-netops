   202	## 3. `ip-vrf` / `evpn` (routed EVPN over SRv6)
   203	
   204	**Status: not achievable on this image.** The EVPN control-plane session
   205	forms and EVPN AF configuration is accepted, but no EVPN-derived forwarding
   206	state can be produced for a L3VNI service, and every honest attempt at an
   207	EVPN-signalled dataplane shows 100% client loss.
   208	
   209	Approaches to try (written before the first attempt):
   210	
   211	- EVPN Type-5 (IP-prefix) routes carrying the per-VRF SRv6 SID, reusing the
   212	  iBGP L2VPN EVPN AF session the gate already reaches Established across
   213	  leaf-spine-leaf; the session today asserts no route of any type.
   214	- Per-VRF `End.DT46` decapsulation as in §2, with the SID learnt through EVPN
   215	  signalling instead of VPN signalling.
   216	- Ingress encapsulation as in §2 (kernel `seg6` on the `vs` ASIC).
   217	- Same client attachment and traffic bar as §2, so the two routed variants
   218	  differ only in signalling.
   219	
   220	| item | verdict | proofs | note |
   221	| --- | --- | --- | --- |
   222	| L2VPN EVPN AF session across leaf-spine-leaf | proven (session only) | `spike-evpn3-state-leaf01`, `spike-evpn2-state-leaf01` | 3/3 peers Established — but pfxRcd 0 for any L3VNI attempt |
   223	| L3VNI → bgpd handoff (Type-5 precondition) | not achievable | `spike-evpn3-state-leaf01`, `spike-evpn3-state-leaf02` | zebra knows the kernel L3VNI (`vxlan-1001`, tenant Vrf1) but bgpd's VNI view reports `Number of L3 VNIs: 0` — the zebra→bgpd VNI ZAPI handoff never delivers the L3VNI, so no Type-5 route can be generated |
   224	| IPv6 Type-5 export knobs in `vrf Vrf1` AF6 | not achievable | `spike-evpn3-kl006` | `sid vpn export auto` / `rd vpn export` / `rt vpn both` stick, but `network <prefix>` is `% Unknown command` inside the VRF IPv6 AF; the EVPN table stays empty (KL-006 recorded as upstream-moot here) |
   225	| dataplane without BGP state (RT-leak attempt) | not achievable | `spike-evpn3-ping` | 100% loss both with only leaked default-table state and with ONLY EVPN signalling standing (knobs removed); the leaked subnets are a config smell the honest attempt also exposes |
   226	| restore of the §2 as-built after the attempts | proven | `spike-evpn3-ping` (leg C), `spike-evpn3-rollback` | steering routes + decap back, client traffic 0% loss again |
   227	| verdict for the renderer | **not achievable** | all of the above | `srv6-supported.yaml` refuses `ip-vrf`+`evpn` by name |

   229	## 4. `mac-vrf` / `evpn` (bridged EVPN over SRv6)
   230	
   231	**Status: deferred by owner decision 1; the measured attempts are recorded
   232	here anyway.** Bridged (L2VNI) signalling between the leaves works; the
   233	bridged service itself is not deliverable on the as-built lab.
   234	
   235	Approaches to try (written before the first attempt):
   236	
   237	- EVPN Type-2/3 MAC/IP routes over SRv6 with a per-EVI or per-(EVI, VLAN)
   238	  End.DX2-style SID — the SRv6 equivalent of the VXLAN bridged service; SID
   239	  scope per EVI or per VLAN is a spike question.
   240	- The operator-supplied VLAN tag (as in feature 003) carried on the attachment
   241	  ports, bridged into the EVI.
   242	- Kernel bridging plus `seg6` encapsulation on the `vs` ASIC as the dataplane
   243	  candidate, as in §2.
