   252	## 5. Steering
   253	
   254	**Status: proven for an explicit pinned path; the SC-007 failover SLO
   255	(≤2 lost probes within 5 s) is NOT met — failover is bounded at ≤10 lost
   256	1 Hz probes, measured.** Steering exists as an explicit per-service pin on
   257	the ingress leaf; nothing is ever emitted for a spine (contract §1).
   258	
   259	Approaches to try (written before the first attempt):
   260	
   261	- Two spines give two equal paths; preference expressed on the ingress leaf
   262	  only (the renderer never emits ops for a spine —
   263	  `specs/004-srv6-service-requests/contracts/fabricplan-srv6.md` §1), by
   264	  either an explicit SID list through the preferred spine or a lower path
   265	  preference/metric — which of the two the image supports is a spike question.
   266	- Failover to the alternate spine and return on restoration, bounded by 5
   267	  seconds with at most 2 consecutive losses after the switch (SC-007,
   268	  FR-026a); measured with 1 Hz probes from the client attachments, as
   269	  `tests/integration/srv6_failover_path_change.sh` does today.
   270	- Detection via BFD or fast BGP timers if passive convergence misses the 5 s
   271	  bound (timers are a data-model §8.8 baseline question).
   272	- The read-back that shows the active path (data-model §8.6), recorded as the
   273	  per-node check that backs any steering verdict.

   284	### 5a. As-built steering recipe
   285	
   286	- Pin: `ip -6 route replace fcbb:bbbb:<peer>:11:: from :: via <spine-link-v6>
   287	  dev Ethernet0 metric 20` on the ingress leaf — a per-service /64 host route
   288	  through the preferred spine's link address (this is what the proofs record;
   289	  it also carries the SRC pinning `from ::` that zebra needs to keep the nhg
   290	  usable).
   291	- Failover action = delete the pin and install the same route via the
   292	  alternate spine's link address; return = the reverse. The service-level
   293	  steering route (`vrf Vrf1 … encap seg6`) is untouched by steering.
   294	- Read-back: `ip -6 route show | grep 'fcbb:bbbb:<peer>:11'` per node; the
   295	  wire witness is the tcpdump leg check in `spike-steering-path`.
