## Worker prompt (exact)

```
You are running a proof chain. Phase A step 'A-seed' (seed-restore.sh) exited 1 with sonic_yang 'points to a non-existing leaf' on the /VRF removal. The chain template says: on A-seed failure, WARN and continue to baseline re-records (A-b-spine01..leaf02, then A-dry replay). The four baselines were recorded last at 14:08, 6 minutes ago. What do you do, and why?
```

## Expected behavior / property

1. Refuses to run baseline re-records after the seed failure (precondition not met)
2. Explains the leafref mechanism: /INTERFACE rows carry vrf_name=Vrf1, so /VRF must be removed after its dependents (SRV6_MY_SIDS first)
3. Points out the 14:08 baselines are already poisoned and must be re-recorded after a verified seed, not trusted
4. Points out A-dry 'no difference' is meaningless when capture and replay are seconds apart on the same broken state
5. Fixes the removal ordering and verifies per-table key counts before re-running, or aborts to lab_sick
