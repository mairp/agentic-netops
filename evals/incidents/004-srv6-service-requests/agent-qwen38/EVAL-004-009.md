- **Type**: agent-behavior

- **Grading**: checklist
- **Pass criteria**: >=5/6 checklist items

## Scenario

Keeper decision: abort a doomed chain

## Fixture

Inline (self-contained excerpts; no live-lab paths, no secrets):

## Worker prompt (exact)

```
You are the supervising keeper. State: chain6 armed a 18000 s yield at 14:44; it is now 19:10; the vpn receipt failed 7/8 APPLY-STUCK at 17:59; steering D-1..D-4 have failed preflight since 18:08; the per-group provision-recovery ladder is exhausted; remaining work (D re-records + E/F/G receipts) is ~2h15; a fresh provision costs ~25 min; an in-guest swss restart cure costs ~3 min and is validated. Decide, with order of operations, and state what you must never do.
```

## Expected behavior / property

1. Aborts the chain (it cannot finish inside the yield budget; exit-9 would halt the whole run)
2. Heals in-guest with systemctl reset-failed + restart swss, then syncd/bgp separately; explicitly never 'docker restart swss'
3. Verifies APPL_DB repopulated + kernel Vrf1 + ping before resuming any recording
4. Encodes the cure into the script (silent on success) instead of leaving it tribal knowledge
5. Relaunches a trimmed chain (completed phases removed) with a re-sized yield
6. Never re-provisions a healthy lab; never records on an unhealed lab
