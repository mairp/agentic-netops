     3  # T062 Metrics Inventory and Labels
     4  
     5  This document inventories metrics from the pinned SONiC schema, SDC, provider, Kubernetes, containerlab, and SRv6 MySID counters. It defines bounded labels, topology joins, and required dashboards/alerts.
     6  
     7  Sources and examples (series names are indicative; exporters must label consistently):
     8  
     9  - SONiC (via gNMIc OTLP):
    10    - sonic_interface_packets_total{device,interface,dir}
    11    - sonic_interface_octets_total{device,interface,dir}
    12    - sonic_bgp_session_state{device,neighbor,state}
    13    - sonic_srv6_mysid_packets_total{device,sid,behavior}
    14  - SDC:
    15    - sdc_target_reachable{target}
    16    - sdc_config_apply_total{target,result}
    17    - sdc_deviation_total{target,path}
    18  - Provider (Go Prom client):
    19    - ainetops_sonicprovider_applies_total{}
    20    - controller_runtime_reconcile_errors_total{controller}
    21  - Kubernetes:
    22    - up{job}
    23    - kube_pod_status_ready{namespace,pod}
    24  - gNMIc exporter:
    25    - gnmic_output_errors_total{output}
    26    - gnmic_subscribe_errors_total{target}
    27  - OTel Collector:
    28    - otelcol_exporter_queue_size{exporter}
    29    - otelcol_exporter_enqueue_failed{exporter}
    30    - otelcol_exporter_sent_metric_points{exporter}
    31  
    32  Bounded labels and joins:
    33  - device: one of {spine01, spine02, leaf01, leaf02}
    34  - interface: Ethernet[0-9]+ on SONiC; joined to containerlab link map to derive peer
    35  - neighbor: IPv4/IPv6 address string; joined to device by BGP sessions from intent
    36  - sid: SRv6 endpoint SID; joined to SRv6Service intent
    37  - pod/namespace: Kubernetes topology
    38  - link (derived): nodeA:ifA<->nodeB:ifB constructed from containerlab inspect
    39  
    40  Topology join:
    41  - ConfigMap deploy/observability/topology-configmap.yaml contains nodes/links that can be loaded by Grafana Flow to render a physical fabric view and join series by {device,interface} labels.
    42  
    43  Dashboards/alerts mapping:
    44  - Physical fabric view (Grafana Flow): rate/utilization using sonic_interface_* metrics; node/link status bound to up and sonic_* state counters.
    45  - SRv6 Service Path: MySID packet counters and active primary/alternate path state.
    46  - Pipeline health: receiver/exporter health, queue fill, dropped/refused points, subscribe errors.
    47  - Alerts: See deploy/observability/rules/ainetops.rules.yaml and prometheus.yaml rules.
