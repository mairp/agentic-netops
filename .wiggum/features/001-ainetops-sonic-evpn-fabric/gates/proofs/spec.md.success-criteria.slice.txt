    16	  reason within two reconciliation intervals (default 30s), with no false aggregate Ready state.
    17	- **SC-008**: Prometheus has healthy targets for the provider, SRv6 controller, SDC, gNMIc,
    18	  OTel Collector, and all qualified SONiC telemetry sources; Grafana loads the provisioned
    19	  fabric and orchestration dashboards without manual datasource setup.
