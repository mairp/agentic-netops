     1	## Scope and interpretation
     2	
     3	"Replace a Cisco router with SONiC" means replace its **logical role and supported service
     4	intent** with a SONiC leaf, border leaf, or spine. It does not mean SONiC can run on arbitrary
     5	router hardware or that every WAN/MPLS feature has an EVPN equivalent. The MVP covers a
     6	containerlab fabric and only the mappings listed in this specification. It contains no
     7	Crosswork, NSO, CNC, proprietary NED, or vendor-specific controller dependency.
     8	
     9	### Non-goals — the multi-agent intent tier
    10	
    11	This specification covers the **declarative control plane only**: the fabric, its resources,
    12	and the controllers that reconcile them. It deliberately excludes the subject project's
    13	**multi-agent intent tier** — the conversational supervisor and its specialist worker agents
    14	that turn a natural-language service request into structured intent, described in `REVERSE.md`
    15	§1, §2, and §5.
    16	
    17	That exclusion was applied when this feature replaced the vendor-specific use case, but was
    18	never written down. Recording it here closes that gap. Two points of clarification, since the
    19	omission has been misread as the tier being overlooked:
    20	
    21	- **The reverse-engineering analysis did not miss it.** `REVERSE.md` documents the tier in
    22	  detail, including its transport, protocol, gateway port, and the division of labour across the
    23	  four roles.
    24	- **The exclusion is a scope boundary, not a rejection.** `research.md` Decision 2 rejects only
    25	  a specific arrangement — an agent holding a device session, with no durable Kubernetes desired
    26	  state, weaker validation, and unclear field ownership. It does not reject an intent tier that
    27	  sits *above* the declarative boundary and submits resources through the cluster API like any
    28	  other client.
    29	
    30	The tier is specified separately in [`specs/002-agntcy-intent-tier/spec.md`](../002-agntcy-intent-tier/spec.md),
    31	which layers on top of this feature and depends on it. That specification adds no requirement to
    32	this one, changes no resource schema, controller, or reconciliation contract here, and is
    33	required to leave every gate in this feature passing with the tier removed. Nothing in this
    34	specification needs to change for it to land.
