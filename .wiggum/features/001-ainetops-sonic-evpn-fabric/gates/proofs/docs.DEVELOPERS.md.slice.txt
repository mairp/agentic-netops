     1	# AINETOPS Developers Guide
     2	
     3	This document describes development workflows and responsibilities for the AINETOPS SONiC EVPN/VXLAN Fabric implementation.
     4	
     5	- Build binaries: make build
     6	- Run envtest: make test-envtest
     7	- Static checks: make test-static
     8	- Verify pins and API shapes: make verify-compat
     9	- Supply-chain checks: make supply-chain; CI also runs deny-list and provenance
    10	
    11	## RBAC and field ownership
    12	
    13	- Provider uses server-side apply with field manager "ainetops-sonic-provider"
    14	- ClusterRole scopes are minimal: events, Kubenet NetworkDevice/status, SDC Config/Target
    15	- Do not widen verbs without justification
    16	
    17	## Logging and redaction
    18	
    19	- Do not log secrets, usernames, or passwords
    20	- Emit events with reason strings from pkg/reasons; use standard Condition types
    21	
    22	## Reproducibility
    23	
    24	- All container images are built with CGO_ENABLED=0 (static) and use distroless:nonroot runtime
    25	- Avoid reliance on local state; render deterministically from inputs
    26	
    27	## Deny-list policy
    28	
    29	- See README for policy summary; run make denylist locally to reproduce CI policy
    30
