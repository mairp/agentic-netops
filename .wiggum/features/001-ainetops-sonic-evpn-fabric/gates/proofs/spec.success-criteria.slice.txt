   346	  is neither overwritten nor claimed.

   383	## Assumptions
   384	
   385	- The target is a lab and reference architecture, not proof that virtual SONiC matches every
   386	  production ASIC or hardware platform.
   387	- Kind is a disposable lab control plane. Required evidence must be exported before `off.sh`;
   388	  PVC persistence is scoped to the lifetime of the Kind environment unless the operator uses
   389	  the documented evidence-preservation option.
   390	- A complete Kubenet release/commit and its matching CRDs/examples will be selected during
   391	  implementation; tutorial and main-branch APIs are not assumed interchangeable.
   392	- A SONiC image containing the required management and EVPN functions is legally obtainable
   393	  and can be pinned. If `sonic-vs` fails EVPN or SRv6 qualification, `sonic-vm` or another
   394	  pinned SONiC profile that passes the unchanged gate is the conformance target.
